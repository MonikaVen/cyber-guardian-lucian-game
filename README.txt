Cyber Guardian — Lucian's Adventure

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

This version uses PNG assets for:
- Lucian the Cat — playable hero
- Vesper the Wolf — identity & access guardian
- Mira the Rabbit — security analyst
- Finn the Fox — network scout
- Bramble the Hedgehog — incident investigator
- Pip the Goblin — suspicious outsider

Design choices:
- only two low-noise backgrounds: magical forest and cyber castle
- subtle falling leaves
- animated incoming email letters
- lightweight JS network paths and nodes
- 10 cybersecurity chapters inspired by personal-data safety, phishing, MFA, supplier risk, NIS2/DORA resilience, incident response, critical services, and recovery
- accessibility options: reduced motion, no camera shake, email animations toggle
